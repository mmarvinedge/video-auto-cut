Privacy Policy

video-auto-cut · Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who this applies to

video-auto-cut is a personal, self-hosted tool that runs only on its owner's computer. Its only users are the owners of the TikTok and YouTube accounts that connect to it. It has no servers, website accounts or analytics.

2. Data the app receives from TikTok

When an account is connected, the app receives only:

  • the account's TikTok identifier (open_id), username and display name;
  • access and refresh tokens, which the app needs to upload videos;
  • creator settings needed for posting, such as the allowed privacy levels and whether comments, duets or stitches are turned off.

The app does not read followers, messages, contacts, other people's videos or any other account data.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload videos that the account owner has confirmed and to show the connected account and post status in the app's panel. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

  • All data is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local folder that is never published or synced by the app.
  • The app sends data only to TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com), and only to perform the actions above.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. To delete the data:

  • revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; and
  • remove the account in the app's panel (Accounts tab). This deletes the stored tokens and account details from the computer.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may change. The current version is always on this page. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/mmarvinedge/video-auto-cut/issues.

Política de Privacidade

O video-auto-cut é uma ferramenta pessoal que roda só no computador de quem o usa. Não tem servidores, cadastro nem analytics.

Quando uma conta do TikTok é conectada, o app recebe só três coisas: o identificador da conta, o nome de usuário e o nome de exibição; os tokens de acesso; e as configurações de postagem (privacidades permitidas e interações desligadas). Ele não lê seguidores, mensagens nem outros dados. Essas informações servem só para publicar os vídeos que o dono da conta confirmou e para mostrar o status das publicações no painel.

Tudo fica guardado apenas no computador do dono e só é enviado para a API oficial do TikTok. Nada é vendido nem compartilhado com terceiros. Para apagar os dados, remova o acesso do app nas configurações do TikTok e remova a conta na aba Contas do painel. Dúvidas ou pedidos de exclusão podem ser enviados pelas issues do repositório.
