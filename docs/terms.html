video-auto-cut · Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. What the app is

video-auto-cut is a personal, self-hosted tool. It finds highlights in recorded live streams, turns them into short vertical videos and, when its owner chooses to, uploads those videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It runs only on its owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no user sign-ups.

2. Using TikTok with the app

  • The app connects to a TikTok account only after the account owner logs in through TikTok's official authorization page and approves the requested permissions.
  • Every upload is reviewed and confirmed by the account owner in the app's panel. Before each post the owner chooses the privacy level, the interaction settings and the commercial content disclosure.
  • Uploads must follow TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Usage Confirmation.

3. Responsibility for content

The account owner is responsible for the videos they publish. That includes having permission from the streamers whose content is clipped, and following copyright law and each platform's rules.

4. Disconnecting

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is revoked, the app stops working with that account.

These terms may change. The current version is always on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/mmarvinedge/video-auto-cut/issues.
